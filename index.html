Hello! I am Robiul.

I am a economics researcher with experience in development research, education, child development, migration, and environmental and resource economics.

I have worked as a Research Assistant on studies of Rohingya refugee and host-community populations under the supervision of Md. Johirul Islam, formerly at the BRAC Institute of Governance and Development (BIGD), BRAC University. My work has included field surveys, data management, statistical analysis, and manuscript preparation.

I studied Economics at the University of Chittagong , Bangladesh, in the Bachelor of Social Sciences (Honours) programme. I have also contributed to research on graduate labour markets and fisheries management in the Bay of Bengal region.

My research manuscripts explore child development, educational inequality, labour market uncertainty, and household decision-making. See my research and teaching for details.

Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of Development Economics, Behavioral Economics, and Data & Computational Science . I am particularly interested in understanding how economic conditions, social institutions, and individual decision-making shape outcomes for vulnerable populations in developing countries.

My research uses quantitative and empirical approaches to investigate these questions, with a particular emphasis on:

•

Experimental & Causal Research — evaluating policies and interventions using experimental and quasi-experimental methods.

•

Economic & Mathematical Modeling — applying economic theory, econometric methods, and mathematical models to understand complex social and economic phenomena.

•

Data & Computational Methods — using statistical programming, data analysis, and computational tools to generate evidence-based insights.

Focus Areas: Child development and marriage, social protection, poverty and inequality, social networks, intimate partner violence, women's empowerment, education, mental health, microcredit, parenting, and household decision-making.
