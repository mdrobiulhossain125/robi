Hello! I am Robiul.

I am a economics researcher with experience in development research, education, child development, migration and environmental and resource economics.

I have worked as a Research Assistant on studies of Rohingya refugee and host-community populations under the supervision of Md. Johirul Islam, formerly at the BRAC Institute of Governance and Development (BIGD), BRAC University. My work has included field surveys, data management, statistical analysis and manuscript preparation.

I studied Economics at the University of Chittagong, Bangladesh, in the Bachelor of Social Sciences (Honours) programme. I have also contributed to research on graduate labour markets and fisheries management in the Bay of Bengal region.

My research manuscripts explore child development, educational inequality, labour market uncertainty and household decision-making. See my research and teaching for details.

Research Interests

My research interest lies at the intersection of Development Economics, Data and Computational Science. I am passionate about addressing challenges in developing countries through:

  • Economic/Mathematical Modeling
  • Data and Computational Tools

Focus Areas: Child marriage, social protection, poverty, social networks, intimate partner violence, empowerment, education, mental health, microcredit, and parenting.

Perceived rationality pushes each of us toward self-interest; self-interest, aggregated, can wreck the very system we depend on (Braess's paradox). The usual fix is mechanism design, but the designers are human too. Does the paradox recurse forever, and is there a single condition under which the system still holds?
